About Us

SURFEELA is a Peshawar-based manufacturer of quality cleaning products. We make two brands — Surfeela and Muneela — serving homes, businesses and distributors across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

FAQ

Yes. Wholesale and distributor prices apply automatically based on quantity (10+ wholesale, 50+ distributor).

How do I place an order?

Add products to cart, fill your details and click "Place Order via WhatsApp".

What payment methods?

Cash on Delivery and Easypaisa (payment verified manually).

How long is delivery?

Usually 2–4 working days depending on city.
